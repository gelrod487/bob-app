# Founding 75 — 18 personal messages (with word tracks)

How to use this
- Send them yourself, one at a time, from your own phone or account. Personal beats mass.
- Swap in [Name], and add one real detail about them (a deal they closed, a conversation you had).
- The goal of the FIRST message is only a reply: "yes, send it" or a tour click. Don't pitch the whole offer up front.
- Always say "sample data" when you mention the tour numbers.
- Links: tour https://getbob.agency/demo.html  |  agency tour https://getbob.agency/demo.html?mode=agency
  |  sign up https://getbob.agency  |  support@getbob.agency

The offer in one line
First 75 agents to start a free 14-day trial (no card) lock in 50% off every plan for as long as they remain paid subscription members
subscribed. Producer $14.50 (reg. $29), Producer Plus $19.50 (reg. $39), Agency $39.50 (reg. $79).

Cadence for every person
- Day 0: first message.   Day 3: one short follow-up.   Day 7: one last, friendly note. Then stop.
- If they click the tour but don't sign up, ask what they'd want to see.

## Word tracks for the replies you'll get

"Is it a CRM?"
- "No. It's not a CRM. It tracks the money: cases, commissions, chargebacks, lead costs, and what you net. Use it
  next to whatever CRM you have."

"Is my clients' information safe?"
- "You don't have to enter anything sensitive: no Social Security or bank numbers. A name, phone, email and the
  case details are enough. The data is yours, and I'll export or delete it any time you ask."

"What's the catch? What does it cost?"
- "No catch. The 14-day trial needs no card. If you stay, the Founding rate is 50% off for as long as you remain a paid subscription member: Producer is $14.50 a month, Producer Plus $19.50."

"I already use a spreadsheet."
- "That's what BOB replaces. It can import your spreadsheet, and then it does the net-profit math, the vendor
  ROI and the date ranges for you."

"I'm too busy."
- "Fair. Setup is about 10 minutes, and I'll do it with you on a call. Want the 2-minute tour first? No
  signup."

"Does it work for annuities and IUL, or just final expense?"
- "All of it: final expense, term, whole life, IUL and annuities. Annuities are tracked by deposit."

"Can I try it with my team?"
- "Yes. The Agency plan shows each producer's production and your override income. Each agent starts their own
  free trial. Here's the agency tour: getbob.agency/demo.html?mode=agency"
