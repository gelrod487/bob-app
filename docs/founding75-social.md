### Comment replies (save these)
- "Is it a CRM?" It's not. It tracks the money: cases, commissions, chargebacks, lead costs and net profit. Use it
  next to your CRM.
- "How much?" Free for 14 days with no card. Founding members get 50% off: Producer $14.50/mo.
- "Is my data safe?" You don't enter anything sensitive: no SSNs or bank numbers. The data is yours, and I'll export
  or delete it on request.

LinkedIn tips: post between 7 and 9 am on weekdays, keep the first two lines strong (they show before "see
more"), put the link in the post or the first comment, reply to every comment within a few hours, and use a
personal profile photo, because people follow people.
