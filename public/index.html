FAQ

Questions producers actually ask.

Who is BOB built for?

Solo producers running their own book, and agency owners tracking production and cost across a whole team. Same ledger underneath — the Agency plan just adds staff costs, overrides, and roll-up numbers across everyone.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

Sign up with your email — no card required. You get full access to every feature for 14 days. After that, you'll need to subscribe to keep using your book; nothing you've entered is lost while you decide.

Can I import my existing spreadsheet?

Yes. Upload a CSV or Excel file, match its columns to BOB's fields with a simple mapping screen, and your clients, cases, and commission history come in without retyping everything.

How does BOB handle chargebacks?

Every case tracks issue-paid FYC alongside any chargebacks against it, so your net profit always reflects commissions minus chargebacks minus real costs — not just gross commission.

Is there a contract, or can I cancel anytime?

No contract. Cancel anytime from Settings — you can export your book first, so you keep your data even after you go.

Is my data private?

Your book is tied to your account only — it's not a shared spreadsheet, and other producers or agencies can never see your clients, commissions, or numbers.

Does BOB integrate with a specific carrier or CRM?

No — BOB is independent software built for any licensed producer, regardless of carrier. It's not affiliated with or limited to any one company.
